In short

  • AnotherKeepass has no account, no server of its own and no telemetry.
  • Your vaults are files on your device or wherever you put them. I, the developer, have no access to them.
  • The app only makes the connections described below. Almost all of them can be switched off.

The app

What is stored on your device

  • Vaults (.kdbx): encrypted with your master password and optionally a key file or YubiKey. They live wherever you save them.
  • Settings and the list of recent vaults: in the app's profile on your device.
  • Log file: events such as start, opening a vault and errors, without passwords or entry contents. It never leaves your device unless you send it with a bug report.
  • Windows Hello: for quick unlock a key stays in memory only, never on disk.

No data is sent to me or to third parties unless you trigger it yourself.

This website

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes data needed to deliver the page, such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Version shown: the page asks the GitHub API for the newest version and download links. GitHub sees your IP address.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no third-party fonts. The font is served by this website itself.

Microsoft Store

If you get AnotherKeepass from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes purchase, download and update data under the Microsoft privacy statement. I only receive aggregated figures such as the number of installs.

Your rights

As the app sends no personal data to me, I hold no data about you. If you email me or open a GitHub issue, I use what you send only to answer. Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correction, deletion and to complain to a data protection authority.
